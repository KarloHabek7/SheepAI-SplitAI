# I07: Multi-Stakeholder — Resident × Tourist × City Worker

> **Category:** I_PERSONA (Persona-Driven Sprint)
> **Time Box:** 12 min
> **Recommended Model:** Opus 4.6 (Thinking)
> **Depends On:** R04 (Citizen Complaints)
> **Priority:** 🟡 High

## Objective

Generate ideas from **three distinct personas simultaneously** — a Split resident, a foreign tourist, and a city worker (komunalni redar). The best ideas serve all three. Ask: "What ONE platform could make all three of these people's lives easier?"

## Creative Lens

Embody each persona in sequence, then find the intersection.

### Persona 1: Marija (Local Resident, 45, Varoš)
- Works in tourism hospitality, lives in the old town
- Frustrated by noise every summer night, overflowing bins, impossible parking
- Uses Gradsko oko but feels nothing ever happens with her reports
- Wishes the city actually listened

### Persona 2: Thomas (German Tourist, 32, First Visit)
- Arrives by ferry, overwhelmed by Split's beauty but confused by logistics
- Can't figure out bus routes, parking zones, waste disposal rules at Airbnb
- Doesn't speak Croatian, Google Translate is unreliable for official rules
- Wants a personal guide that understands Split's quirks

### Persona 3: Ivan (Komunalni Redar, 38, City Worker)
- Drowning in Gradsko oko reports — 200+ per week, most low-priority
- Spends half his shift driving to complaints that were already resolved
- Wishes someone would pre-sort and prioritize his daily route
- Needs photos classified before he arrives on scene

## Output Format

Write to `Research/Findings/I07_multi_stakeholder_findings.md`:

### Sprint Angle
Ideas that serve the intersection of resident, tourist, and city worker needs.

### Ideas Generated (4–5 ideas)

For each idea:
- **Name**
- **One-line pitch** (≤15 words)
- **How it serves Marija** (resident value)
- **How it serves Thomas** (tourist value)
- **How it serves Ivan** (city worker value)
- **Core AI Features** (max 3)
- **Why the intersection matters** (what's better about serving all 3?)
- **Killer Demo Moment**
- **Scoring:** Feasibility, Demo-ability, AI Depth, City Alignment, Uniqueness (1–5 each)
