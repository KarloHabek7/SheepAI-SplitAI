# I06: Gap-Filler — What's Missing in Split's Digital Ecosystem?

> **Category:** I_GAP (Gap-Filler Sprint)
> **Time Box:** 12 min
> **Recommended Model:** Opus 4.6 (Thinking)
> **Depends On:** R05 (System Integration Map), R06 (EU Benchmarks)
> **Priority:** 🟡 High

## Objective

Using the system integration map (R05) and competitor analysis (R06), identify the biggest GAPS in Split's digital ecosystem and generate ideas that fill them. Ask: "What do Barcelona/Helsinki citizens have that Split citizens don't? How could AI bridge that gap?"

## Creative Lens

You are a frustrated Split resident who just moved from Barcelona. You miss the seamless digital services. Design what you wish existed.

## Input Files to Read First
- `Research/Findings/R05_system_integration_findings.md` — What Split has
- `Research/Findings/R06_competitor_analysis_findings.md` — What others have
- `docs/research/R03_tech_data_findings.md` — Pre-existing tech landscape

## Output Format

Write to `Research/Findings/I06_gap_filler_findings.md`:

### Sprint Angle
Filling the gaps between Split's fragmented systems and EU best practices.

### Ideas Generated (4–5 ideas)

For each idea:
- **Name**
- **One-line pitch** (≤15 words)
- **Gap Being Filled** (what's missing that other cities have?)
- **Target User Persona**
- **Core Features** (max 3)
- **Integration Points** (which existing Split systems does it unify?)
- **Modular Expansion Path** (how does it grow beyond MVP?)
- **Killer Demo Moment**
- **Scoring:** Feasibility, Demo-ability, AI Depth, City Alignment, Uniqueness (1–5 each)
