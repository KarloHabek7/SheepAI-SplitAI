# R06: EU Smart City Benchmarks & Competitor Solutions

> **Category:** R_COMP (Competitor Analysis)
> **Time Box:** 15 minutes
> **Recommended Model:** Gemini 3.1 Pro High
> **Depends On:** None
> **Priority:** 🟡 High

## Objective

Research what other European cities (especially Mediterranean/tourism-heavy cities comparable to Split) have built for AI-powered municipal services. Identify solutions that work, features that resonate, and gaps that no one has filled yet. This gives us a competitive landscape to differentiate against.

## Search Strategy

### Tier 1: Direct Competitors (Mediterranean Tourism Cities)
1. **Dubrovnik** — "Dubrovnik smart city" / "Dubrovnik tourist management AI"
   - How do they manage tourist overcrowding in the Old Town?
   - Any AI/digital tools for crowd control?
2. **Barcelona** — "Barcelona Decidim" / "Barcelona smart city platform" / "Barcelona AI municipal"
   - Decidim participatory platform, Sentilo IoT platform
   - What AI tools do they use for civic engagement?
3. **Venice** — "Venice smart city tourist management" / "Venice crowd control technology"
   - How do they manage over-tourism with technology?
   - Day-tripper fee system, crowd monitoring
4. **Lisbon** — "Lisbon smart city AI" / "Lisboa Inteligente"
   - Smart city dashboard, transit integration
5. **Athens** — "Athens smart city" / "Athens digital municipality"

### Tier 2: AI-First Municipal Solutions (Global)
1. **Helsinki** — "Helsinki AI city services"
   - Known for progressive digital services
2. **Singapore** — "Singapore smart nation municipal AI"
   - Advanced AI for urban management
3. **Tallinn** — "Tallinn e-government"
   - Digital-first government services
4. **Seoul** — "Seoul smart city AI complaints"
   - AI-powered citizen complaint processing

### Tier 3: Specific Solution Categories
1. **AI Municipal Chatbots** — "city AI chatbot municipal services" / "government RAG chatbot"
   - Who has built one? How well does it work? What are the limitations?
2. **Computer Vision for Civic Issues** — "AI pothole detection city" / "AI waste classification municipal"
   - Who uses Vision AI for issue reporting?
3. **Unified City Apps** — "super app city services" / "one-stop city digital platform"
   - Who has successfully unified fragmented city services?

### What to Capture for Each Solution:
- **City & Solution Name**
- **What it does** (1–2 sentences)
- **Technology stack** (if known)
- **User reception** (positive? Complaints?)
- **What works well** (features to emulate)
- **What's missing** (gaps we could fill)
- **Applicability to Split** (High/Med/Low)
