# I05: Gemini-Maximal — What Can ONLY AI Do?

> **Category:** I_TECH (Tech-Driven Sprint)
> **Time Box:** 12 min
> **Recommended Model:** Opus 4.6 (Thinking)
> **Depends On:** R07 (Gemini Capability Audit)
> **Priority:** 🔴 Critical

## Objective

Generate ideas that **maximally leverage Gemini's unique capabilities** — especially multimodal, grounding, and function calling. These ideas should be IMPOSSIBLE without AI. Ask: "What could Split residents/tourists do with Gemini that they literally cannot do today?"

## Creative Lens

You are a Gemini API power user who knows every capability. Design experiences that would be science fiction without AI but are trivially buildable with Gemini.

## Input Files to Read First
- `Research/Findings/R07_gemini_audit_findings.md` — What Gemini can actually do
- `docs/research/pre_hackathon_mock_idea_reference.md` — Context on Split

## Output Format

Write to `Research/Findings/I05_gemini_maximal_findings.md`:

### Sprint Angle
Ideas that are impossible without AI — pushing Gemini to its limits.

### Ideas Generated (4–5 ideas)

For each idea:
- **Name** (catchy)
- **One-line pitch** (≤15 words)
- **Why it NEEDS AI** (what makes it impossible without Gemini?)
- **Target User Persona**
- **Gemini Features Used** (be specific: which model, which capability)
- **Input → Output Flow** (what goes in, what comes out)
- **Killer Demo Moment**
- **Scoring:** Feasibility, Demo-ability, AI Depth, City Alignment, Uniqueness (1–5 each)

### Cross-Pollination Notes
- Which ideas combine well with seed variants (I04)?

## Out of Scope
- Do NOT pick a winner
- Do NOT write code
