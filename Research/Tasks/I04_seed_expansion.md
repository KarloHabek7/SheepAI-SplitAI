# I04: Seed Expansion — 5 Variants of SplitAI

> **Category:** I_SEED (Seed Expansion Sprint)
> **Time Box:** 12 min
> **Recommended Model:** Opus 4.6 (Thinking)
> **Depends On:** R04 (Citizen Complaints), R05 (System Integration Map)
> **Priority:** 🔴 Critical

## Objective

Take the existing SplitAI concept (RAG + Vision municipal agent) and **diverge it into 5 meaningfully different variants**. Each variant should emphasize a different core capability, target user, or architecture. The goal is not to pick one — it's to explore the full design space around the seed idea.

## Creative Lens

You are a product architect who loves the SplitAI concept but believes it could be even better. For each variant, ask: "What if SplitAI was PRIMARILY about ___?"

## Input Files to Read First
- `docs/research/pre_hackathon_mock_idea_reference.md` — The seed idea
- `Research/Findings/R04_citizen_complaints_findings.md` — Real pain points
- `Research/Findings/R05_system_integration_findings.md` — What systems exist

## Output Format

Write to `Research/Findings/I04_seed_expansion_findings.md`:

### Sprint Angle
Expanding the SplitAI seed in 5 different architectural/thematic directions.

### Ideas Generated (5 variants)

For each variant:
- **Variant Name** (catchy, memorable)
- **One-line pitch** (≤15 words)
- **Primary Focus** (what makes this variant DIFFERENT from the seed)
- **Target User Persona** (be specific: age, occupation, scenario)
- **Core AI Features** (max 3)
- **Integration Strategy** (which existing Split systems does it connect to?)
- **Modular Architecture** (how would it expand post-hackathon?)
- **Killer Demo Moment** (the 10-second clip that wows judges)
- **Scoring:**
  - Feasibility (1–5)
  - Demo-ability (1–5)
  - AI Depth (1–5)
  - City Alignment (1–5)
  - Uniqueness (1–5)

### Cross-Pollination Notes
- Which variants could combine?
- Which features from different variants are complementary?

## Out of Scope
- Do NOT pick a winner — that's `/ideate`
- Do NOT write code
