# I07: Multi-Stakeholder — Ideation Results

> **Executed:** 2026-05-16T11:00:00Z
> **Creative Angle:** Multi-Stakeholder Intersection (Resident, Tourist, City Worker)
> **Agent Model:** Gemini 3.1 Pro (High)
> **Research Inputs Used:** R04, R02, I01

## Sprint Angle
Ideas that serve the exact intersection of resident frustrations, tourist confusion, and city worker overload. The goal is a unified platform where solving one persona's problem actively mitigates the pain points of the other two.

## Ideas Generated

### Idea 1: Split Unified Triage & Guide AI (SmartCity Hub)
- **Pitch:** AI command center that guides tourists, resolves citizen reports, and routes city workers.
- **How it serves Marija:** Takes a photo of a problem, and the AI instantly classifies it and gives a realistic ETA based on the worker's current route.
- **How it serves Thomas:** Multilingual conversational AI answers his logistics questions (parking, waste), preventing him from accidentally causing issues.
- **How it serves Ivan:** Gets an optimized, pre-classified route of verified issues, filtering out duplicates and noise from the 200+ weekly reports.
- **Core AI Features:** Multimodal LLM (image classification for reports), Multilingual NLP (tourist Q&A), Routing Optimization Algorithm.
- **Why the intersection matters:** Educating the tourist prevents the exact infrastructure strain that frustrates the resident and overworks the city worker.
- **Killer Demo Moment:** Marija uploads a photo of illegal parking; the system groups it with 5 identical reports, Ivan's dashboard updates his route, and the system replies to Marija: "Ivan is 12 mins away."
- **Scoring:** Feasibility: 4, Demo-ability: 5, AI Depth: 4, City Alignment: 5, Uniqueness: 4

### Idea 2: Smart Split Sound & Sentiment
- **Pitch:** AI predicts and prevents noise and public order issues using real-time anomaly data.
- **How it serves Marija:** Her neighborhood stays quieter because the system detects escalating noise and sends wardens before a party gets out of hand.
- **How it serves Thomas:** Receives friendly, automated AI notifications in his language about quiet hours and Zona A rules.
- **How it serves Ivan:** Dashboard highlights predicted trouble zones based on sound spikes and social media density, optimizing his night patrols.
- **Core AI Features:** Audio anomaly detection, predictive sentiment/crowd analysis, automated multilingual nudges.
- **Why the intersection matters:** Proactive tourist education and predictive routing directly solve the #1 resident pain point (party tourism noise).
- **Killer Demo Moment:** Simulating a noise spike in Varoš; the AI triggers a WhatsApp nudge to registered tourist numbers in the area about quiet hours, while Ivan's heat map flashes red for deployment.
- **Scoring:** Feasibility: 3, Demo-ability: 5, AI Depth: 5, City Alignment: 5, Uniqueness: 5

### Idea 3: Omni-Transit & Access Predictor
- **Pitch:** AI mobility platform syncing buses, parking, and pedestrian flow for seamless movement.
- **How it serves Marija:** Knows exactly when the bus arrives and avoids gridlocked streets near the ferry port with AI-predicted traffic data.
- **How it serves Thomas:** Gets real-time, translated transit ETAs and available parking directions straight to his Airbnb.
- **How it serves Ivan:** Receives alerts on illegal parking hotspots that are actively blocking bus routes, allowing targeted enforcement.
- **Core AI Features:** Time-series forecasting for transit ETAs, computer vision for parking availability, dynamic rerouting.
- **Why the intersection matters:** Better transit info for tourists means fewer rental cars clogging Marija's street, while Ivan enforces parking where it matters most.
- **Killer Demo Moment:** Thomas asks the AI for the fastest way to Diocletian's Palace; the AI suggests a Park & Ride because it detects severe central congestion, freeing up a spot for Marija.
- **Scoring:** Feasibility: 4, Demo-ability: 4, AI Depth: 4, City Alignment: 5, Uniqueness: 3

### Idea 4: Dynamic Waste & Resource Oracle
- **Pitch:** AI anticipates waste accumulation and coordinates smart disposal for everyone.
- **How it serves Marija:** Enjoys a cleaner street because bins are emptied right before they overflow based on predictive scheduling.
- **How it serves Thomas:** Scans a QR code in his Airbnb to ask the AI exactly how and where to recycle specific items in his native language.
- **How it serves Ivan:** Spends zero time verifying "full bin" reports because AI schedules pickups predictively based on tourist density and historical data.
- **Core AI Features:** Predictive demand modeling (based on tourist numbers/weather), multilingual visual QA (for sorting).
- **Why the intersection matters:** Educating tourists on sorting prevents the mess locals hate, and predictive models keep workers from chasing empty bins.
- **Killer Demo Moment:** Thomas uploads a photo of a plastic bottle; AI tells him the exact bin location. The system predicts the bin will be full in 2 hours and adds it to Ivan's route.
- **Scoring:** Feasibility: 4, Demo-ability: 4, AI Depth: 3, City Alignment: 5, Uniqueness: 4
