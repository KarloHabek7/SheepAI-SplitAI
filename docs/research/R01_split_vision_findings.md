## Executive Summary
- **Massive Tech & Innovation Investment:** Over **€10 million** is actively being invested in public administration computerization and smart city initiatives aimed at solving real-life citizen situations (as of February 2026).
- **Upcoming AI Center & Assistant:** More than **€2 million** has been allocated specifically to establish the **Centar za umjetnu inteligenciju (AI Center)**, with further plans for an administrative **AI Assistant** to guide both municipal clerks and citizens.
- **Eco-Friendly Green Leadership:** Split is the first city in Croatia to join the **EBRD Green Cities** initiative, accelerating its transition to sustainable urban mobility and green infrastructure by 2030.
- **Comprehensive Strategic Core:** The approved "Strategija razvoja grada Splita do 2030" relies on four structural arenas: **Društvo (Society)**, **Gospodarstvo (Economy)**, **Urbano okruženje (Urban Environment)**, and **Institucionalni okvir (Institutional Framework)**.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Društvo (Society & Affordable Living)
- **Affordable Housing Focus:** Housing accessibility is a primary focus. The city plans to build ~300 apartments in Mejaši, Kamen, and Ravne Njive, coupled with a state-partnership project in Korešnica providing ~350 units in the first phase. Expected realization: by 2028.
- **Inclusivity & Demographic Growth:** Initiatives emphasize modernizing schools, building new kindergartens, and providing systematic support to reduce poverty risks.

### 2. Gospodarstvo & Digitalizacija (Economy & Tech Infrastructure)
- **The Split Smart City Ecosystem:** An integrated platform and mobile app already allow for fee-free bill payments, digital tracking of student scholarships, subsidies for entrepreneurs, and cultural funding requests.
- **Establishment of the AI Center:** Split is actively moving towards becoming a regional AI hub, investing €2 million to cultivate local talent, private-public technology innovation, and modernized public governance tools.

### 3. Urbano okruženje & Mobilnost (Urban Environment & Green Cities)
- **Inteligentni transportni sustav (ITS):** Major efforts in smart traffic systems at intersections to optimize traffic flows in real-time.
- **Smart Split Parking:** Widespread utilization of sensors synced directly to citizen mobile apps for immediate detection of empty spaces.
- **Green Cadastre & Environmental Efficiency:** Joining EBRD Green Cities brings prioritized cataloging of green assets and measures targeting urban resilience to climate change.

### 4. Institucionalni okvir (Transparency & Partnerships)
- **Open Governance:** Heavy emphasis on participatory budgeting, transparent distribution of public assets (like digital licensing of public terraces), and swift digital interfaces to modernize citizen interactions with local authorities.

## Implications for Ideation
- **Build toward:** Conversational or proactive **AI-driven navigation tools** that plug into existing municipal applications to simplify interactions with local administration. The city explicitly plans an AI assistant, offering direct alignment.
- **Avoid:** Heavy infrastructure hardware projects (e.g. road sensors) as these are already governed by large ITS contracts. Direct AI analytics or high-level orchestration software provides far higher fast-build potential.
- **Opportunity gap:** Melding the **EBRD Green Cities** push with the new **AI Center**. Applications could focus on AI-empowered analysis of urban heat islands, green cadastre reporting, or citizen-facing environmental impact trackers.
- **Technical enabler:** The dedicated €2 million backing for the **AI Center** proves Split has highly receptive municipal stakeholders for cutting-edge AI integrations, providing strong rationale for selecting highly visual, AI-first architectures for the hackathon MVP.
